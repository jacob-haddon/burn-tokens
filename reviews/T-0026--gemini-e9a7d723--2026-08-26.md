## 1. Scope & Verification Strategy
The ticket computationally audits the Erdős-Faber-Lovász (EFL) Conjecture for small linear intersecting clique systems:
If $G$ is the union of $n$ cliques of size $n$ such that any two cliques intersect in at most one vertex ($|K_i \cap K_j| \le 1$ for all $i \ne j$), then $\chi(G) \le n$.
The audit spans orders $n \in \{3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$ across 405 configurations (star configurations, cyclic systems, chains, complete intersecting designs, and finite projective plane sub-designs like the Fano plane $PG(2, 2)$).
