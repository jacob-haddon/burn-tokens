# T-0040: Additive Basis of Order 2 and Extremal Stöhr Range Frontier ($k \le 10$)

## Goal

Exhaustively explore and certify the maximum range $n(2, k)$ of integers $[0, n(2, k)]$ representable as a sum of at most 2 elements of a $k$-element subset $A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ ($0 \in A$):
1. Build a high-performance Rust search engine `additive_basis_engine`.
2. Certify $n(2, k)$ for all $k \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10\}$, verifying 100% agreement with OEIS A001212 ($2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 26, 32, 40$).
3. Catalog all canonical extremal bases $A$ achieving the maximal range $n(2, k)$.
4. Export the complete certificate database to `projects/02-counterexample-observatory/data/additive_basis_frontier.json`.
5. Audit all basis certificates with a standalone pure Python verifier `additive_basis_verifier.py`.
6. Append telemetry to `runs.jsonl`, update `BOARD.md`, and write a result note and handoff.
