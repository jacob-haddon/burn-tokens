# T-0035: Formalization of the Coproduct Universal Property of Commutative Monoids in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. The canonical inclusion homomorphisms $\iota_1 : M \to M \times N$ and $\iota_2 : N \to M \times N$.
2. The universal copairing map $[f, g] : M \times N \to P$ for any pair of homomorphisms $f : M \to P$ and $g : N \to P$ into a commutative monoid $P$.
3. The triangle commutation identities $[f, g] \circ \iota_1 = f$ and $[f, g] \circ \iota_2 = g$.
4. The categorical coproduct uniqueness: any homomorphism $h : M \times N \to P$ satisfying $h \circ \iota_1 = f$ and $h \circ \iota_2 = g$ is strictly equal to $[f, g]$.
5. Machine-checked with 0 `sorry` declarations and standard foundational core axioms (`[propext, Quot.sound]`).
