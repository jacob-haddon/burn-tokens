# Ticket T-0048: Formalization of Frobenius Reciprocity & Modular Galois Connections

## 1. Context & Frontier
Formalize the key structural theorems from Goswami, Janelidze, and Manuell (*Applied Categorical Structures*, 2025; arXiv:2502.06010):
1. **Frobenius Reciprocity on Lattices**: For adjoint monotone maps $f \dashv g$ between bounded lattices $L_1, L_2$, $f(a \wedge g(b)) = f(a) \wedge b$.
2. **Modular Adjunction Characterization**: Equivalence of Frobenius reciprocity, modular law compatibility on interval sublattices, and the Galois-induced lattice isomorphism theorem.
3. **Interval Isomorphisms**: Machine-checked proof that the restriction of $f$ and $g$ yields an order isomorphism between interval sublattices.
