# T-0046: Additive Energy Spectrum & Extremal Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers Frontier ($|A| \le 8$)

## Goal

Exhaustively explore and certify the exact discrete spectrum of additive energy $E(A) = |\{(a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4) \in A^4 : a_1 + a_2 = a_3 + a_4\}|$ for all finite subsets $A \subset \mathbb{Z}$ of cardinality $|A| \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$:
1. Verify theoretical bounds across all orders: $E_{\min}(k) = 2k^2 - k$ (achieved by Sidon/dissociated sets) and $E_{\max}(k) = \frac{2k^3 + k}{3}$ (achieved by arithmetic progressions).
2. Characterize the full set of realizable intermediate energy values and canonical witness subsets.
3. Export the complete machine-readable certificate dataset in JSON format to `projects/02-counterexample-observatory/data/additive_energy_frontier.json`.
4. Audit all energy certificates with a standalone pure Python verifier `verify_additive_energy.py`.
5. Author result note adhering to `AGENTS.md` format.
