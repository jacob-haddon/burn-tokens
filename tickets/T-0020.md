# T-0020: Monoid Direct Product & Categorical Universal Property in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. **Direct Product Monoid**: Cartesian product $M \times N$ of monoids $(M, \cdot, 1_M)$ and $(N, \cdot, 1_N)$ with componentwise multiplication and unit $(1_M, 1_N)$.
2. **Coordinate Projections**: The projections $\pi_1 : M \times N \to M$ and $\pi_2 : M \times N \to N$ are valid monoid homomorphisms.
3. **Categorical Universal Property**: For any monoid $P$ and homomorphisms $f : P \to M$ and $g : P \to N$, there exists a unique homomorphism $\langle f, g \rangle : P \to M \times N$ such that $\pi_1 \circ \langle f, g \rangle = f$ and $\pi_2 \circ \langle f, g \rangle = g$.
4. **Commutativity Characterization**: $M \times N$ is commutative $\iff$ both $M$ and $N$ are commutative.
5. **Product Associativity & Symmetry Isomorphisms**: $(M \times N) \times P \cong M \times (N \times P)$ and $M \times N \cong N \times M$ as monoids.
6. Zero `sorry` declarations and standard core Lean 4 axioms (`[Quot.sound]`).
