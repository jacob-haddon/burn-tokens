# T-0036: Submonoid Lattice Structure and Closure Systems in Lean 4

## Goal

Produce a self-contained, machine-checked formalization in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies) of:
1. **Submonoid Definition**: Structure `Submonoid M` with predicate closure under multiplication and identity.
2. **Infimum / Intersection**: Arbitrary intersection $\bigcap_{i} S_i$ is a submonoid.
3. **Generated Submonoids & Inductive Words**: The closure $\langle X \rangle$ generated by a subset $X \subseteq M$.
4. **Moore Closure Operator**: Proofs that $\langle \cdot \rangle$ is extensive ($X \subseteq \langle X \rangle$), monotone ($X \subseteq Y \implies \langle X \rangle \subseteq \langle Y \rangle$), and idempotent ($\langle \langle X \rangle \rangle = \langle X \rangle$).
5. **Complete Lattice Join**: Proof that the supremum $\bigvee_{i} S_i = \langle \bigcup_{i} S_i \rangle$ satisfies the universal join axioms.
6. Zero `sorry` declarations and standard foundational core axioms.
