# T-0015: Galois Connections, Closure Operators, and Subposet Adjunctions in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize a foundational, self-contained Lean 4 theory of **Galois connections between partially ordered sets** with zero `sorry` and standard axioms. Prove unit/counit adjunction inequalities, triangular cancellation identities, closure and kernel operator properties of the composite endomorphisms $g \circ f$ and $f \circ g$, and the mutual order-isomorphism between the subposets of closed and open elements.
