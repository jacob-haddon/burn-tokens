# T-0039: Formalization of Cyclic Groups, Order Exponent Congruence, and Power Division Theorem in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. **Group Structure & Power Exponentiation**: General `MyGroup` algebraic structure with natural power exponentiation $g^k$ satisfying $g^0 = 1$, $g^{k+1} = g \cdot g^k$.
2. **Exponent Arithmetic & Commutativity**:
   - `npow_add`: $g^{a+b} = g^a \cdot g^b$
   - `npow_mul`: $g^{a \cdot b} = (g^a)^b$
   - `cyclic_comm`: $g^a \cdot g^b = g^b \cdot g^a$ (every cyclic group is abelian).
3. **Element Order & Power Congruence**:
   - Predicate $\text{IsFiniteOrder } g\ n$ ($n > 0$, $g^n = 1$, and $g^k \ne 1$ for $0 < k < n$).
   - `pow_rem_eq_one`: $g^n = 1 \land g^k = 1 \implies g^{k \pmod n} = 1$.
   - `order_dvd_of_pow_eq_one`: $g^k = 1 \implies n \mid k$.
   - `pow_eq_one_iff_dvd`: $g^k = 1 \iff n \mid k$.
   - `pow_eq_pow_iff_dvd_sub`: $g^a = g^b \iff n \mid (a - b)$ for $a \ge b$.
4. **Homomorphic Image Functoriality**:
   - The image of any cyclic group under a group homomorphism is cyclic (`image_cyclic_is_cyclic`).
5. Zero `sorry` declarations and standard foundational core axioms (`[propext]`).
