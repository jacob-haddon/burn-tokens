# T-0021: Optimal Golomb Ruler Frontier & Difference Triangle Exact Certification ($n \le 11$)

## Goal

Exhaustively compute and certify the exact optimal Golomb ruler lengths $O(1)=0, O(2)=1, O(3)=3, O(4)=6, O(5)=11, O(6)=17, O(7)=25, O(8)=34, O(9)=44, O(10)=55, O(11)=72$ (matching OEIS A003006). Prove existence of valid rulers at length $L = O(n)$ and exhaustive non-existence at length $L < O(n)$. Catalog all canonical optimal rulers and their difference triangles in a machine-readable JSON artifact and independently verify distinct difference properties in pure Python.
