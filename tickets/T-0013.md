# T-0013: Schur Numbers and Sum-Free Partition Finite Frontier ($k \le 4$, $S(k) \le 44$)

## Goal

Exhaustively compute and certify the classical Schur numbers $S(1)=1, S(2)=4, S(3)=13, S(4)=44$ (OEIS A030126) and weak Schur numbers $WS(1..3)$, proving existence of sum-free $k$-partitions for $N = S(k)$ and non-existence for $N = S(k) + 1$. Catalog all canonical extremal sum-free partition configurations in a machine-readable JSON artifact and independently verify sum-freeness ($x + y \ne z$) in pure Python.
