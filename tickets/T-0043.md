# T-0043: Formalization of Monoid Ideals, Rees Congruences, and Rees Quotient Monoids in Lean 4

## Goal

Produce a self-contained, machine-checked formalization in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies) of:
1. **Monoid Ideal Structure**: `MonoidIdeal M` defining subsets $I \subseteq M$ satisfying $m \cdot x \in I$ and $x \cdot m \in I$ for all $m \in M, x \in I$.
2. **Rees Congruence Compatibility**: Formulate the Rees relation $x \sim_I y \iff x = y \lor (x \in I \land y \in I)$ and prove it is an equivalence relation and compatible monoid congruence `reesCongruence I`.
3. **Rees Quotient Monoid**: Construct the quotient monoid $M / \rho_I$ (`ReesQuotient I`) with canonical multiplication and identity element $\llbracket 1 \rrbracket$.
4. **Canonical Projection & Zero Absorption**:
   - The canonical projection homomorphism `reesProj I : MyMonoidHom M (ReesQuotient I)`.
   - Absorption theorem: For any $i_0 \in I$, the equivalence class $\mathbf{0}_I = \llbracket i_0 \rrbracket$ acts as a two-sided absorbing zero: $\forall q \in M/I, q \cdot \mathbf{0}_I = \mathbf{0}_I \land \mathbf{0}_I \cdot q = \mathbf{0}_I$.
5. **Universal Factorization Property**: For any monoid homomorphism $f : M \to N$ sending all elements of $I$ to a two-sided zero $0_N \in N$, construct the unique factoring homomorphism `reesLift f h_zero`.
6. Zero `sorry` declarations and standard foundational core axioms (`[propext, Quot.sound]`).
