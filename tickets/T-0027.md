# T-0027: Formalization of the First Isomorphism Theorem for Monoids in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. The **Monoid Congruence** structure `MonoidCongruence M` (equivalence relations compatible with multiplication).
2. The **Quotient Monoid** instance `instMyMonoidQuotient` on `Quotient R.toSetoid`.
3. The **Canonical Projection Homomorphism** `projHom : MyMonoidHom M (Quotient R.toSetoid)`.
4. The **Kernel Congruence** `kerCongruence f` for any monoid homomorphism $f : M \to N$.
5. The **Quotient Universal Property Factorization** `quotLift f h_ker`.
6. The **First Isomorphism Theorem**: The canonical map $\bar{f} : M / \ker(f) \to \text{Im}(f)$ is an isomorphism of monoids (bijective homomorphism), with zero `sorry` declarations and standard core foundational axioms (`[propext, Quot.sound]`).
