# T-0045: Formalization of Green's Relations, Commutation of D-Classes, and Green's Lemma in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. **Green's Five Relations**: $\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{R}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{J}, \mathcal{D}$ on any monoid $M$.
2. **Equivalence Properties**: Prove $\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{R}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{J}$ are equivalence relations.
3. **Green's Commutation Theorem**: Prove relational commutation $\mathcal{L} \circ \mathcal{R} = \mathcal{R} \circ \mathcal{L}$, implying $\mathcal{D}$ is an equivalence relation.
4. **Green's Lemma**: For $a \mathcal{R} b$ witnessed by $a s = b$ and $b t = a$, the right-translation $\rho_s : x \mapsto x s$ restricts to a bijection from the $\mathcal{L}$-class of $a$ to the $\mathcal{L}$-class of $b$, with inverse $\rho_t : y \mapsto y t$, and preserves $\mathcal{H}$-classes ($H_a \cong H_b$).
5. Machine-checked with 0 `sorry` declarations and standard foundational core axioms (`[propext, Quot.sound]`).
