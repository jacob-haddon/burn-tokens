# T-0029: Distributive Lattice Equational Duality & Boolean Complementation Unicity in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. **Lattice & Distributive Lattice Typeclasses**: Formulate algebraic lattice axioms (associativity, commutativity, idempotency, absorption) and prove mutual equivalence of meet-over-join distributivity $a \wedge (b \vee c) = (a \wedge b) \vee (a \wedge c)$ and join-over-meet distributivity $a \vee (b \wedge c) = (a \vee b) \wedge (a \vee c)$.
2. **Bounded Distributive Lattices**: Introduce bottom $\bot$ and top $\top$ identity elements.
3. **Strict Complementation Unicity**: Prove that if an element $a$ has a complement $b$ ($a \wedge b = \bot$ and $a \vee b = \top$), then $b$ is unique ($b_1 = b_2$).
4. **Boolean Algebra & De Morgan Duality**: Constructive involution $\neg (\neg a) = a$ and De Morgan identities $\neg (a \vee b) = \neg a \wedge \neg b$, $\neg (a \wedge b) = \neg a \vee \neg b$.
5. Zero `sorry` declarations and 0 custom axioms.
