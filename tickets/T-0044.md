# T-0044: Graham-Sloane Harmonious Tree Conjecture Finite Certificate Generator ($n \le 12$)

## Goal

Exhaustively generate and certify harmonious labelings for all non-isomorphic canonical trees up to $n = 12$. For each tree $T = (V, E)$ on $n$ vertices with $m = n - 1$ edges, construct a labeling $f : V \to \mathbb{Z}_m$ such that the induced modular edge sums $f^*(uv) = (f(u) + f(v)) \bmod m$ are all distinct and bijectively cover $\{0, 1, \dots, m-1\}$. Verify that the Graham-Sloane conjecture holds across 100% of tested trees with exactly **0 counterexamples**, export the full JSON certificate dataset, and audit with an independent pure Python verifier.
