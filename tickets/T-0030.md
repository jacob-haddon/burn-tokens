# T-0030: Erdős-Szemerédi Sum-Product Trade-Off & Additive-Multiplicative Energy Frontier ($|A| \le 7$)

## Goal

Exhaustively explore and certify the exact finite Erdős-Szemerédi sum-product frontier $M_k = \min_{|A|=k} \max(|A+A|, |A\cdot A|)$ and energy trade-off for subset sizes $k \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$:
1. Build a high-performance Rust exploration engine `sum_product_engine` in `projects/02-counterexample-observatory/sum_product_engine/`.
2. Compute the exact minimum envelope $M_k = \min_{|A|=k} \max(|A+A|, |A\cdot A|)$ and catalog all extremal minimizing subsets $A \subset \mathbb{Z}$.
3. Compute exact additive energy $E_+(A) = |\{(a,b,c,d) \in A^4 \mid a+b=c+d\}|$ and multiplicative energy $E_\times(A) = |\{(a,b,c,d) \in A^4 \mid ab=cd\}|$.
4. Export full machine-readable results to `projects/02-counterexample-observatory/data/sum_product_frontier.json`.
5. Build an independent pure Python verifier `sum_product_verifier.py` auditing all sum/product sets and energy counts from scratch.
6. Record metrics in `runs.jsonl`, update `BOARD.md`, and write a result note and handoff.
