# T-0047: Formalization of the Semidirect Product of Monoids, Action Compatibility, and Split Projections in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. **Monoid Action Structure**: `MonoidAction (N M : Type _)` representing actions of monoid $N$ on monoid $M$ by monoid endomorphisms $\alpha : N \to \text{End}(M)$, satisfying:
   - $\alpha(n)(m_1 \cdot m_2) = \alpha(n)(m_1) \cdot \alpha(n)(m_2)$
   - $\alpha(n)(1) = 1$
   - $\alpha(n_1 \cdot n_2)(m) = \alpha(n_1)(\alpha(n_2)(m))$
   - $\alpha(1)(m) = m$
2. **Semidirect Product Type**: `SemidirectProduct M N \alpha` with multiplication $(m_1, n_1) \cdot (m_2, n_2) = (m_1 \cdot \alpha(n_1)(m_2), n_1 \cdot n_2)$ and identity $(1, 1)$.
3. **Monoid Instance**: Prove `MyMonoid (SemidirectProduct M N \alpha)` (associativity, left identity, right identity).
4. **Canonical Inclusions & Projection**:
   - `inlHom : MyMonoidHom M (SemidirectProduct M N \alpha)` mapping $m \mapsto (m, 1)$.
   - `inrHom : MyMonoidHom N (SemidirectProduct M N \alpha)` mapping $n \mapsto (1, n)$.
   - `projN : MyMonoidHom (SemidirectProduct M N \alpha) N` mapping $(m, n) \mapsto n$.
5. **Split Exact & Intertwining Identities**:
   - `projN ∘ inrHom = id_N`.
   - `projN ∘ inlHom = 1`.
   - Fundamental commutation law: $\text{inrHom}(n) \cdot \text{inlHom}(m) = \text{inlHom}(\alpha(n)(m)) \cdot \text{inrHom}(n)$.
6. Machine-checked with 0 `sorry` declarations and standard foundational core axioms (`[propext, Quot.sound]`).
