# T-0041: Formalization of the Grothendieck Group Construction and Universal Property in Lean 4

## Goal

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):
1. The **Grothendieck Congruence Relation** on $M \times M$ for a commutative additive monoid $M$: $(a, b) \sim (c, d) \iff \exists k \in M, a + d + k = c + b + k$.
2. The **Grothendieck Abelian Group Structure** `GrothendieckGroup M := Quotient (grothendieckSetoid M)` with addition, zero $\overline{(0, 0)}$, and inverse $-\overline{(a, b)} = \overline{(b, a)}$.
3. The **Canonical Monoid Homomorphism** $\iota : M \to \mathcal{K}(M)$ with $m \mapsto \overline{(m, 0)}$.
4. The **Universal Property of the Grothendieck Group**: For any abelian group $G$ and monoid homomorphism $f : M \to G$, there exists a unique group homomorphism $\bar{f} : \mathcal{K}(M) \to G$ such that $\bar{f} \circ \iota = f$.
5. Machine-checked with 0 `sorry` declarations and standard foundational core axioms (`[propext, Quot.sound]`).
