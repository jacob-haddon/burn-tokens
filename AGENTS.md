# Autonomous Research Lab: Frontier Discovery & Novelty Contract

This repository is dedicated **strictly to genuine scientific discoveries, open conjecture frontiers, and non-trivial formalizations**. Unattended agents must never spend compute on textbook exercises or duplicate solved theorems.

---

## 🚫 Strictly Prohibited (Zero-Tolerance Anti-Triviality Rule)

Agents MUST NOT propose, execute, or promote:
- ❌ **Standard Undergraduate Algebra**: Basic group/monoid axioms, division algorithm, Bézout identity, elementary Chinese Remainder Theorem, basic modular arithmetic.
- ❌ **Standard Mathlib Duplicates**: Theorems that already exist in core Lean 4 Mathlib (unless formalizing a novel 2024–2026 preprint lemma).
- ❌ **Naive / Toy Puzzles**: Trivial finite cases with no scientific citation or OEIS reference.

---

## 🎯 Mandatory High-Value Research Pillars

All tasks in `tickets/` and `proposals/` must belong strictly to one of three research pillars:

1. 🥇 **Open OEIS Sequences & Certified Extrema (`02-counterexample-observatory`)**:
   - Sequences marked *"more terms needed"* or *"conjectured"* in OEIS.
   - Generating exact mathematical certificates, extremal graphs, or bound improvements for known open combinatorial sequences.
2. 🥈 **Novel arXiv Preprint Formalization (2024–2026) (`01-open-lean-missions`)**:
   - Formalizing unformalized key lemmas from fresh mathematical papers published on arXiv between 2024 and 2026.
   - Must cite the specific arXiv preprint ID and provide 0 `sorry` machine check.
3. 🥉 **AI Scientific Proof Auditing & Soundness Watchdog (`03-proof-reproduction-watch`)**:
   - Auditing newly published preprint proof packages generated by AI systems, checking for circular reasoning, invalid axioms, or compilation errors.

---

## ⚙️ Resource & Compute Policy (Zero Local Overload)

- **Local CPU Time**: Max **30 seconds** per command (only for Lean compilation, SAT solver verification, or lightweight certificate checking).
- **Zero Heavy Brute Force**: Heavy searches must use algebraic pruning, SAT/SMT encodings, or tight analytical bounds.
- **LLM Reasoning First**: The heavy lifting must be done by Google LLM inference synthesizing tactical proofs and analytical certificates.

---

## 📋 Standard Ticket Lifecycle

1. **Scout Mode**: Propose genuine frontier tasks citing arXiv DOIs or OEIS IDs.
2. **Review First**: Review any pending tickets in `status: review` before executing new tasks.
3. **Execution**: Complete within 2.5 hours, generate result note, handoff, and completion notice.
4. **Independent Verification**: Transition to `status: review` for zero-trust adversarial auditing.
