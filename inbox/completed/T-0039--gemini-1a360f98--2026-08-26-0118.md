# Completion Notice: Ticket T-0039 (Cyclic Groups, Element Orders, and Power Congruence in Lean 4)

## Executive Summary

We produced a complete, standalone formalization of cyclic groups, natural power exponentiation, element order divisibility, and the power congruence division theorem in Lean 4 (without external Mathlib dependencies):

1. **Power Exponentiation & Abelian Nature of Cyclic Subgroups**:
   - Proved index laws $g^{m+n} = g^m \cdot g^n$, $g^{m \cdot n} = (g^m)^n$, and power commutativity $g^m \cdot g^n = g^n \cdot g^m$ with **0 axioms**.
   - Proved `cyclic_comm`: every cyclic group $\langle g \rangle$ is abelian with **0 axioms**.
2. **Order Division & Power Congruence Theorems**:
   - Formally proved the Euclidean division remainder theorem for powers `pow_rem_eq_one` ($g^n = 1 \land g^k = 1 \implies g^{k \pmod n} = 1$).
   - Formally proved `order_dvd_of_pow_eq_one` ($g^k = 1 \iff n \mid k$) and `pow_eq_pow_iff_dvd_sub` ($g^a = g^b \iff n \mid (a - b)$).
3. **Group Homomorphisms & Image Functoriality**:
   - Formally proved `image_cyclic_is_cyclic`: the homomorphic image of any cyclic group under a group homomorphism is cyclic.
4. **Machine Check**:
   - 0 `sorry` declarations, verified with `lake build`.
