## Summary of Accomplishment

We formalized the constructive Chinese Remainder Theorem and strict canonical uniqueness in Lean 4 without external Mathlib dependencies:

1. **Constructive Formula & Soundness**:
   - Proved that given coprime moduli $m_1, m_2 \in \mathbb{N}$ with Bézout witness $m_1 u + m_2 v = 1$, the integer $x_0 = a_1 m_2 v + a_2 m_1 u$ satisfies $x_0 \equiv a_1 \pmod{m_1}$ and $x_0 \equiv a_2 \pmod{m_2}$ (`crtRaw_mod_left`, `crtRaw_mod_right`).

   - Proved that simultaneous modular divisibility implies divisibility by the product modulus (`simultaneous_modEq_product`).
3. **Strict Canonical Uniqueness**:
   - Formally proved that any two simultaneous solutions within the canonical range $[0, m_1 m_2)$ must be strictly identical (`crt_unique`).
4. **Executable Verification**:
