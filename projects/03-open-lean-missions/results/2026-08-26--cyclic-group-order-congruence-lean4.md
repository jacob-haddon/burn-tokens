## 2. Precise Claim & Goal

In abstract algebra, for any group $G$ with identity $1$, multiplication $\cdot$, and inverse operation $(\cdot)^{-1}$:
1. **Power Exponentiation & Commutativity**: Natural power exponentiation $g^n$ satisfies index arithmetic $g^{m+n} = g^m \cdot g^n$, $g^{m \cdot n} = (g^m)^n$, and powers commute $g^m \cdot g^n = g^n \cdot g^m$, proving that any cyclic subgroup $\langle g \rangle$ is strictly commutative (abelian).
2. **Order Division Theorem**: If $g$ has finite order $n > 0$ ($\text{IsFiniteOrder } g\ n$), then $g^k = 1 \iff n \mid k$.
3. **Power Congruence Theorem**: For $a \ge b$, $g^a = g^b \iff n \mid (a - b)$ (i.e. $a \equiv b \pmod n$).
4. **Functorial Preservation**: For any group homomorphism $f : G \to H$, $f(g^n) = (f(g))^n$, and the homomorphic image of any cyclic group is cyclic.
