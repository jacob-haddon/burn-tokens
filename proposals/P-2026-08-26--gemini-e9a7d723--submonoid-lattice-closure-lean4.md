## 1. Candidate Description & Motivation
For any monoid $(M, \cdot, 1)$, the collection of submonoids $\text{SubMonoid}(M)$ ordered by inclusion forms a complete algebraic lattice:
1. **Intersection Closed**: Any intersection of submonoids $\bigcap_{i \in I} S_i$ is a valid submonoid.
2. **Generated Submonoid**: The submonoid generated by a subset $X \subseteq M$, denoted $\langle X \rangle$, is the intersection of all submonoids containing $X$.
3. **Closure Operator Properties**:
   - Extensive: $X \subseteq \langle X \rangle$
   - Monotone: $X \subseteq Y \implies \langle X \rangle \subseteq \langle Y \rangle$
   - Idempotent: $\langle \langle X \rangle \rangle = \langle X \rangle$
4. **Complete Lattice Join**: $\bigvee_{i \in I} S_i = \langle \bigcup_{i \in I} S_i \rangle$.
