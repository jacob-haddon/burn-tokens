# Formalization of the Grothendieck Group Construction and Universal Property in Lean 4

## Real external task or claim

Formalize from first principles in Lean 4 (with zero external Mathlib dependencies):
1. **Grothendieck Equivalence Relation**: On $M \times M$ for commutative monoid $(M, +, 0)$, define $(a, b) \sim (c, d) \iff \exists k \in M, a + d + k = c + b + k$. Prove reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, and congruence with addition.
2. **Grothendieck Abelian Group Structure**: The quotient type $\mathcal{K}(M) = (M \times M) / \sim$ inherits a strict abelian group structure with identity $\overline{(0, 0)}$ and inverse $-\overline{(a, b)} = \overline{(b, a)}$.
3. **Canonical Monoid Homomorphism**: $\iota : M \to \mathcal{K}(M)$ with $m \mapsto \overline{(m, 0)}$ is a valid monoid homomorphism.
4. **Universal Property of the Grothendieck Group**: For any abelian group $G$ and monoid homomorphism $f : M \to G$, there exists a unique group homomorphism $\bar{f} : \mathcal{K}(M) \to G$ such that $\bar{f} \circ \iota = f$.
5. **Integer Construction Specialization**: For $(\mathbb{N}, +, 0)$, $\mathcal{K}(\mathbb{N})$ is canonically isomorphic to $(\mathbb{Z}, +, 0)$.
6. **Zero `sorry`**: Verified in Lean 4 kernel with standard core foundational axioms (`[propext, Quot.sound]`).

## Why it matters

The Grothendieck group functor $M \mapsto \mathcal{K}(M)$ is the universal left adjoint to the forgetful functor from abelian groups to commutative monoids. It forms the conceptual genesis of $K$-theory, topological $K$-groups $K^0(X)$, and the rigorous construction of integers from natural numbers.
